Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026 · App: Mi Apunte (com.miapunte.app) · Developer: SgrySoft

🔐

Summary

Mi Apunte does not collect, store or share any personal data. All of your information (notes, transactions, invoices, reminders, settings) is stored exclusively in a local database on your device. The app works 100% offline.

1.Data we collect

None. Mi Apunte does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal data, and does not transmit information to our own or third-party servers.

2.Where your data lives

Everything you create (notes, folders, categories, expenses, income, budgets, invoices, reminders, templates and generated images) is stored in the app's private storage on your device. No one but you has access to it.

3.Backups

The "Export Backup" feature creates a copy of your database that you decide where to save or who to share it with. That file remains under your exclusive control; the app does not send it to any service.

4.Permissions used by the app

  • Notifications (Android): to show the reminders you schedule. You can revoke this permission at any time in system settings.
  • Scheduled alarms (Android): so reminders fire at the exact time you set.
  • Camera (Android): requested only at the moment you choose to photograph a receipt to extract its data. The app never accesses the camera at any other time or in the background.
  • Photos: only when you pick a receipt image from your gallery, through the system's own picker. The app does not scan your gallery.
  • Storage/files: only when you choose to save or share an image, PDF or backup.

5.Receipt reading (text recognition)

The receipt scanning feature uses on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit). The receipt photo never leaves your phone: it is not sent to any server, ours or third-party. The image is stored in the app's private storage alongside the transaction you created, and is removed if you delete that transaction or the app's data.

To work, Google Play Services may download the text recognition model to your device once. That download is performed by the operating system and contains none of your images or data.

6.Sharing content

When you use the "Share" features (text, image, PDF or backup), the content is sent to the app you choose through your device's share system. That transfer is entirely under your control.

7.Advertising and tracking

Mi Apunte contains no ads, includes no advertising or analytics SDKs, and uses no trackers of any kind.

8.Children

The app does not collect data from any user, including children. It is suitable for all audiences.

9.Data deletion

You can delete all of your information at any time by clearing the app's data in system settings or by uninstalling the app. Since no copies exist on servers, deletion is immediate and complete.

10.Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if an optional cloud sync or cloud AI feature is added in the future), we will publish the updated version on this page and inform you inside the app before any data leaves your device.

11.Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at sgrysoft@gmail.com.
