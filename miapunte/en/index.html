What it does

One app instead of five.

Every feature here exists because it was actually needed — not to pad a list.

📸

Scan the receipt

Photograph a receipt and the app pulls out the merchant, the total and the date, then fills the expense in for you. It reads the text inside your phone: no internet, and the photo goes nowhere.

📝

The note takes the shape you need

Eight types, and each one changes the form: a checklist, a recipe with ingredients and steps, meeting minutes with attendees, a diary entry with a mood, a sectioned document, or just free text.

Turn notes into images

Reminders and calendar

Schedule a reminder and it fires at the exact time, even after a reboot. With a calendar view for the whole month.

Three levels of organisation so you can still find that note six months later. With search, favourites and pinning.

PDF and invoices

Export any note to PDF in one tap. And if you invoice clients, build invoices and quotes with line items and numbering.

Export the entire database whenever you like and keep it wherever you decide. No cloud in the middle, nobody to depend on.

FAQ

What everyone asks.

Does it need internet?

No. Mi Apunte works 100% offline. There's no account to create, nothing to sync and no servers involved. Use it on a plane or out in the countryside with no signal and it behaves exactly the same.

Are my notes and expenses uploaded anywhere?

No. The app does not collect, store or share any personal data. Not even the receipt photo: text recognition runs on the phone itself and the image is never sent to any server. It's all spelled out in the privacy policy.

Any ads, purchases or subscription?

None of the three. Mi Apunte is free, has no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription. It also ships with no advertising or analytics SDKs.

How does receipt scanning work?

You photograph the receipt (or pick one from your gallery) and the app reads the merchant name, the total and the date, then fills the expense in for you. It uses on-device text recognition, so it works without internet. You can always correct any field before saving. This feature is currently Android-only.

Can I lock a note with a password?

Yes, each note individually. A protected note never shows its content in lists or on the home screen, and asks for the password to open. It's optional — use it only on the notes that genuinely need it.

Will I lose my data when I update?

No. Installing a new version over the previous one keeps everything: notes, finances, reminders and settings. What does wipe your data is uninstalling the app. That's why Settings has "Export Backup", which saves a complete copy of the database wherever you want it.

What about the iPhone version?

It's on the way. The app is built with .NET MAUI, which compiles for iOS, so it's a matter of time and store review — not of rewriting it.
